Product appetite system

01 / The opportunity

One hero moment. A full campaign behind it.

A launch needs more than one great image. The same production must create appetite in a feed, explain the product in an ad, and give each location something useful to publish.

A creative concept for turning one food shoot into a campaign across the website, paid ads, social, and local promotions.

02 / The approach

From the first idea
to the next location.

Lead with appetite

Show the bite, the texture, and the person enjoying it. Close product shots establish the food; wider frames bring in the social occasion.

Shoot for every placement

Plan portrait, square, and wide compositions before the shoot. Keep room for an offer, a headline, and a clear next step without covering the food.

Build an asset library

Group stills, short clips, product details, and people shots by product and use. Give paid and organic teams enough variety to test different openings.

Learn from response

Compare creative using the goal of each placement: attention for awareness, qualified visits for consideration, and orders where tracking supports attribution.
