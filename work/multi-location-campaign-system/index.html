Creative concept / 03

Multi-location campaign system

Self-initiated concept by Khantrast Media
Focus

Multi-Location, Content, Local Store Marketing

Created for

Fast food and fast casual brands

Project type

Original concept with AI-generated imagery; not commissioned client work.

01 / The opportunity

A campaign that can travel.

As a brand adds locations, creative requests multiply. Teams need a shared direction that protects recognition while making room for different offers, neighborhoods, and trading priorities.

One recognizable brand idea, with the flexibility each market needs to make it relevant locally.

02 / The approach

From the first idea
to the next location.

Define the essentials

Agree on the product story, visual cues, tone, and non-negotiable brand standards. Make it easy for every team to recognize what must stay consistent.

Design the local options

Create approved spaces for an address, an offer, a local moment, or a store team. Build these variations into the templates rather than asking operators to redesign them.

Make distribution simple

Organize assets by channel, location, date, and objective. Give the people publishing them clear instructions and a manageable approval process.

Share what works

Review response market by market, account for different local conditions, and bring useful findings into the next campaign brief.
