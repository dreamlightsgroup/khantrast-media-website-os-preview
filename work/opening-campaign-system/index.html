Creative concept / 02

Opening campaign system

Self-initiated concept by Khantrast Media
Focus

Openings, Local Store Marketing, Paid Growth

Created for

Fast food and fast casual brands

Project type

Original concept with AI-generated imagery; not commissioned client work.

01 / The opportunity

Build demand before opening day.

An opening has a short window to turn curiosity into visits. A useful campaign gives nearby customers a reason to care, clear information about the location, and a reason to come back.

A launch concept that connects neighborhood awareness, opening-day interest, and the first return visit.

02 / The approach

From the first idea
to the next location.

Introduce the neighborhood

Start with the food, the people behind it, and the location. Build awareness among the people who can realistically visit, with dates and details that stay current.

Make opening day clear

Bring the address, hours, menu, offer terms, and directions into one consistent set of assets. Coordinate paid ads, organic posts, creators, and in-store communication.

Capture the first weeks

Plan fresh content around the opening so the campaign can evolve with real guests and real service. Keep production practical for the team running the restaurant.

Give people a return reason

Follow the opening with product stories and local promotions. Review store traffic, offer response, and repeat visits using the data the operator can reliably provide.
