Privacy notice.

This notice covers khantrastmedia.com and inquiries sent to Khantrast Media.

Information you choose to share

If you contact us, we receive the details you send, which may include your name, email address, company, website, locations, marketing priorities, and message. Please share only information relevant to your inquiry and avoid including sensitive personal information.

How the inquiry form works

On this website, the form prepares an email in your own email application. Entering details alone does not send them to Khantrast Media. You review and send the draft through your email provider. The website does not save form entries in a customer database or in browser storage.

How we use inquiries

We use correspondence to respond, discuss your project, and manage the resulting business relationship. Our business email is handled through Google Workspace. Your own email provider also processes messages you choose to send. Any separate service agreement may explain additional processing required for a client project.

Hosting and cookies

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure it. This website does not add advertising pixels, analytics cookies, or a marketing tracking system. Images, logos, and fonts are served with the site.

Keeping and deleting correspondence

We retain inquiry correspondence as needed to respond, maintain an active business relationship, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. To ask about information you have shared, request a correction, or request deletion, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify the request and retain information where required by law.

Links and updates

Other websites and email services have their own privacy practices. This notice applies to this website and may be updated when its features or data flows change. The date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For questions, email info@khantrastmedia.com.
