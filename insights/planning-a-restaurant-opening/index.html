Opening playbook / 3 min read

Opening day is the beginning.

A Khantrast Media point of view

Opening day gets the attention. The work before it creates demand, and the work after it gives people a reason to come back. Plan all three together.

Start with the neighborhood

A new location is a local business, even when the brand is national. Understand who can visit, how they get there, and what occasions the restaurant can become part of: a workday lunch, a family dinner, or a late-night stop.

Use those details to shape the creative and media plan. A broad awareness campaign can introduce the brand, but people nearby also need practical information: where you are, what you serve, and when the doors open.

Make one source of truth

Confirm the address, hours, opening date, menu, and any offer conditions before the campaign goes live. Keep a shared checklist for organic posts, paid ads, creators, local listings, and in-store materials.

Opening dates can change. Assign one person to update the shared information and notify everyone publishing it. A beautiful campaign loses its value if a customer arrives at a closed door.

Plan content around service

Capture the food before launch, then leave room for the energy of the opening itself. Staff, guests, and the first dishes leaving the kitchen tell a different story from a controlled product shoot.

Keep production realistic for the restaurant team. Agree on filming times, permissions, and the shots that matter most so content capture supports the opening instead of interrupting it.

Build the second visit into the plan

The opening offer is an introduction. Follow it with product stories, useful reminders, and a consistent local presence. Give people a new menu item, a familiar favorite, or another relevant reason to return.

Review the measures the business can support: store visits, orders, offer redemptions, and repeat behavior where available. Compare them with the campaign objective and the location’s operating context. Reach alone cannot tell you whether the opening created lasting demand.
